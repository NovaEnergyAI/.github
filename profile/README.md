# Nova Energy AI 🌱

## Empowering Sustainable Web3 Infrastructure Through Open Source

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> **Note:** Nova Energy AI has been open-sourced as of October 2025. Active development has ended, but these tools remain available for the community to use, fork, and build upon. This repository collection represents 20 months of work toward making Web3, AI, and data center operations more sustainable and transparent.

---

## 🌍 Our Mission

Nova Energy was founded with a vision to bring transparency and accountability to the environmental impact of digital infrastructure. We built comprehensive tools for energy validation, sustainability reporting, and green scoring for Web3 node operators, AI developers, and data center operators.

After spinning out from Protocol Labs in March 2024, we worked to create an ecosystem where every kilowatt of energy and every byte of data contributes to a more sustainable future. While we've concluded active operations, we believe these tools can continue to serve the community's sustainability goals.

## 🚀 What We Built

Nova Energy created a complete platform for Environmental Validation Process (EVP) reporting and sustainability tracking:

### Core Features
- **Green Score Calculator**: Sophisticated environmental impact assessment based on hardware efficiency, energy patterns, renewable procurement, and geographic factors
- **Energy Validation Portal**: Multi-stakeholder workflow for submitting, reviewing, and attesting to energy and environmental data
- **Verifiable Claims**: NFT badges and on-chain attestations for sustainability milestones
- **Web3 Integrations**: Native support for Ethereum Attestation Service, Ceramic Network, and Filecoin ecosystem

## 📦 Open Source Repositories

### [www-nova](https://github.com/NovaEnergyAI/www-nova)
**Frontend Application** • Next.js 14, React, TypeScript  
The main web interface for Nova Energy's EVP reporting and Green Score calculations. Features a comprehensive dashboard for web3 node operators and data centers to track and validate their environmental impact.

### [api-nova](https://github.com/NovaEnergyAI/api-nova)
**Backend API** • Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL  
RESTful API powering data management, report generation, and attestation workflows. Handles integrations with REC procurement service providers and blockchain attestation services.

### [nova-contracts](https://github.com/NovaEnergyAI/nova-contracts)
**Smart Contracts** • Solidity, Hardhat  
On-chain components including NFT achievement badges deployed on Base mainnet and attestation contracts for immutable sustainability records.

### [www-greenscore-demo](https://github.com/NovaEnergyAI/www-greenscore-demo)
**Green Score Demo** • Interactive Calculator  
Standalone demonstration of our Green Score methodology. Good for understanding our sustainability metrics and calculations without full platform setup.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, React, TypeScript, SCSS modules
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL
- **Blockchain**: Solidity, Ethers.js, Hardhat
- **Integrations**: Ceramic Network, Ethereum Attestation Service (EAS)

Nova Energy operated from March 2024 to October 2025, initially spun out from Protocol Labs' Filecoin Green initiative. Our co-founders [Marc Johnson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marc--johnson/) and [Nima Ahmed](https://www.linkedin.com/in/niema/) led the development of these tools with a small, dedicated team committed to making digital infrastructure more sustainable.
